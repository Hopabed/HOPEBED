Privacy Policy

Learn how Hopebed collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information while you use our platform and services.

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Hopebed ("Hopebed," "we," "us," or "our") operates hopebed.in (the "Site"), a platform designed to help users discover verified PG and hostel accommodation listings, currently focused on Navi Mumbai.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we may share it, and the choices available to you regarding your personal information.

Pre-launch and validation phase: Hopebed is currently in a pre-launch and validation phase. Our services are evolving, and we may update this Privacy Policy as our platform grows and new features become available.

1. Information We Collect

At this stage, we collect information in limited ways.

a) Information You Provide Directly

We may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Accommodation requirements and preferences
  • Messages or information you provide when contacting us

This information may be collected when you sign up for updates, contact us directly, submit an enquiry, or communicate with us through WhatsApp, email, LinkedIn, or other communication channels.

b) Information from Property Owners and Operators

If you are a PG, hostel, or accommodation owner/operator, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Business or property name
  • Property location
  • Contact details
  • Property details and accommodation information
  • Other information voluntarily shared for listing or verification purposes

c) Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our Site, limited technical information may be collected through standard hosting or analytics tools, including:

  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • General usage information
  • Basic technical information related to your visit
Important: We do not currently collect payment information or government identity documents through the Site. If this changes in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and take appropriate steps required under applicable law.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Notify you about Hopebed's launch or relevant listings
  • Respond to your enquiries and requests
  • Understand accommodation demand and user preferences
  • Improve Hopebed and its services
  • Facilitate introductions between tenants and property owners
  • Communicate with property owners regarding listing opportunities
  • Verify and manage property-related information
  • Provide updates related to Hopebed's services

At the current stage, some matching and communication may be handled manually, including through WhatsApp or other direct communication channels.

Your privacy matters: We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. How We Share Your Information

We may share limited information when necessary to provide or support our services.

This may include sharing information with:

  • Property owners or operators, when you express interest in a property and agree to an introduction
  • Prospective tenants, when you are a property owner and wish to receive relevant enquiries
  • Service providers, such as website hosting, domain, communication, or technology providers, where necessary to operate our services
  • Government authorities or legal bodies, when required by applicable law

We do not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers for their independent marketing purposes.

4. Communication Channels

Hopebed may communicate with you through:

  • WhatsApp
  • Email
  • Phone calls
  • LinkedIn
  • Other communication channels you use to contact us

We may use these channels to respond to your enquiries, facilitate introductions, provide relevant updates, or communicate about Hopebed's services.

You may opt out of non-essential communications at any time by contacting us directly.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

During our current validation stage, some information may be managed using standard business tools such as email, communication platforms, and internal records. We take reasonable care when handling this information.

You may request deletion of your personal information, subject to any legal or legitimate business requirements that may require us to retain certain information.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent where applicable
  • Opt out of future non-essential communications

To make a request regarding your personal information, please contact us using the details provided below.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online is completely secure. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Hopebed is not intended for individuals under the age of 18.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a minor without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Hopebed may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our services, features, and business operations evolve.

For example, we may update this policy when we introduce:

  • Online bookings
  • Payment functionality
  • User accounts
  • Identity or property verification
  • Additional locations or services

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

We will handle personal information in accordance with applicable laws and regulations, including relevant data protection requirements in India.
